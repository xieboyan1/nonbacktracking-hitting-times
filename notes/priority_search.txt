Other works requiring a distinction
  Altisen--Devismes--Lafourcade--Ponsonnet, AlgoTel 2011, Routage par
    marche aleatoire a listes tabous, HAL hal-00588086. Sec. 2 restricts
    the degree-biased YRW kernel to permitted neighbours. Sec. 3, Fig. 3,
    averages simulated travel to a sink in random unit-disc networks and
    reports reduced hitting times with one-entry memory. Their unfavorable
    result (Fig. 4) concerns byte volume. No fixed-pair counterexample.
    https://perso.limos.fr/~palafour/PAPERS/tlm-tlcn-simu-french.pdf
  Gerbaud--Altisen--Devismes--Lafourcade, Comparison of mean hitting times
    for a degree-biased random walk, Discrete Applied Mathematics 170
    (2014), 104-109, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2014.01.021 . This
    memoryless kernel chooses each neighbour v with weight d(v)^(-1/2).
    The examples compare maxima over vertex pairs and, separately, means
    from individual vertices to a distinguished root. Neither 'tabu' nor
    'backtrack' occurs in the author full text. No FIFO1 correction.
    https://home.mis.u-picardie.fr/~devismes/WWW/rapports/deg_biased.pdf
  ADGL's own Proposition 6 gives counterexamples for other lists/rules,
    excluding FIFO1. Their coauthor's habilitation repeats that distinction.
  Breen et al. Appendix A.1(2) already computes the NB hitting time on the
    exact graph class. In their notation n=p+q, k=p+1, start label p-1 and
    target label p (around the p-edge path from B). Their formula gives
    (4p+3q+1)/6. They compare stationary averages, and the appendix does
    not point out the sign reversal for a fixed pair. Credit the overlap.
  Fasino--Tonetto--Tudisco: second-order hitting equations, stationary
    returns and numerical target access averages. https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14438
  Breen et al., RSA (2023): edge-space Kemeny comparisons, with exceptions
    in a different state space. https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.21144
  Tishby et al.: first hitting defined by self-intersection/dead end,
    https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.08375 . Matsuo--Kawagishi--Ohsaki, random
    regular/configuration-model graphs, https://doi.org/10.1587/comex.2022XBL0146 .
  Neal, nonbacktracking and asymptotic variance for MCMC estimators,
    https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0407281 . A general and closely related
    comparison tool, not an explicit correction to ADGL.
  Bodirsky--Ciardo--Kang, the single Semantic Scholar citation of the
    Breen paper, is about adding/removing edges in ordinary random graphs,
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24143 . A public repository claims a
    counterexample to Question 5 only, a different asymptotic growth claim,
    https://huggingface.co/datasets/PureOne/evie-cubic-nonbacktracking-kemeny .
